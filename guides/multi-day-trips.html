Plan a multi-day trip

Split a route into days, choose where you sleep each night, and save the trip with one route per day.

On this page

Split into days Choose nights Ride to the door Save the trip

Split the route into days

  1. Open your route on the map.
  2. Tap More, then Split into days.
  3. Under Split by, choose Days, Distance or Riding time.
  4. Set how many days, or how far or how long you ride each day.

Each day lists its distance, climbing and riding time. Turn on Start time to give the days dates and arrival times.

Choose where you sleep

  1. Tap Choose nights, or tap the moon marker at the end of a day on the map.
  2. The map moves to the end of that day and shows hotels and campsites within 2 km of it for a bike trip, or 1 km for a trip on foot.
  3. Tap a place to open its details, then tap Sleep here.
  4. Switch to Night 2 and do the same for the next day.

To search further from the day's end, tap the chip under the nights that reads Hotel, Camping and the search distance.

Ride to the door or end on the route

When the place you choose is more than 50 m from the route, the app asks how the day ends.

  • Ride to the door adds the way there to that day. The next day starts at the door and rejoins the route.
  • End on the route keeps the day on the route.

Save the trip

  1. Tap Save trip.
  2. Keep the suggested name or type your own, and choose an icon.
  3. Tap Save trip again.

The trip is in History under Collections, with one route per day in day order. Open a day to ride it like any other route. To change the plan, tap Edit trip on the collection.
