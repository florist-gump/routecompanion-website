Multi-day trips

Split a route into days and choose where you sleep each night. Each day lists its distance, climbing and riding time, and the saved trip holds one route per day.

Read the guide: Plan a multi-day trip

Surface on the route

The route line takes the color of the surface under it, so you can see where the gravel starts before you ride it. The Surface key shows how far the route runs on each surface.
