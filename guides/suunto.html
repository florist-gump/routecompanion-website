Suunto Guide

Connect Route Companion to Suunto

Sending routes to Suunto turns on once Suunto has approved Route Companion. Until then the Suunto option does not appear in the app.

Once it is on, you can send a planned route, with its stops, to your Suunto account from the app.

Connect your Suunto account

  1. In Route Companion, open Settings.
  2. Open Integrations and tap Suunto.
  3. Sign in with your Suunto account and allow access.

When you return to the app, Suunto shows as connected.

Send a route to Suunto

  1. Open or plan a route in Route Companion.
  2. Tap Share.
  3. Under Direct upload, tap Suunto.

Suunto appears in the Share route sheet only after you have connected it. Sending a route directly to a connected service is part of Route Companion Pro.

What arrives in Suunto

  • The route, sent as a GPX file.
  • Your stops, as waypoints with the icon that matches their type.

Suunto has no way to update or delete a route from another app, so each upload adds a new route in your Suunto account.

What it does not do

  • It does not read your workouts or any other data from Suunto.
  • It only sends routes, and only when you choose to send one.

Disconnect

Open Settings, then Integrations, tap Suunto and confirm Disconnect. You can reconnect at any time.
