New in 3.4

Type the ride you want and get a route for it, see the climbs and highlights along the way, and pick the best time to leave.

Describe a route (beta)

In the planner, tap Describe and type the loop or trip you want, like "Zurich to Lucerne via Zug". The planner finds routes for it. You can try it for free.

Climbs and highlights

Your route's details list its climbs, and the viewpoints, lakes, passes and swim spots along the way. The planner shows them under the elevation chart while you draw.

Plan around the weather

The weather for your route runs hour by hour, with rain and wind where you'll be. Departure time suggests when to set off. The hourly weather is part of Pro.

Report wrong opening hours

Found a stop whose hours are off? Tap Report a problem and pick Hours are wrong.
